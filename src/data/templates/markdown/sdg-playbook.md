## 3. Hackathon 3-Minute Presentation Deck Blueprint
Use this slide-by-slide structure to build your deck and pitch to the judging panel:

### Slide 1: The Hook & Human Story (0:00 - 0:45)
- **Visual:** High-quality image or bold statement representing the problem.
- **Narrative:** Introduce a specific persona (e.g. "This is Maya, she lives in a city struggling with air pollution..."). Frame the emotional weight.

### Slide 2: The Solution (0:45 - 1:15)
- **Visual:** Clean screenshot or visual diagram of your app.
- **Narrative:** Describe the solution clearly. "We built [App Name], which allows people like Maya to..." Explain the core value proposition in one sentence.

### Slide 3: Live Tech Demo (1:15 - 2:00)
- **Visual:** Live screen-share of the demo path (the "golden path").
- **Narrative:** Show, don't tell. Execute a clean, bug-free flow: signup -> solve the core task -> show success screen. Banned: wandering off into profile settings or editing passwords.

### Slide 4: SDG Metric Board & Impact (2:00 - 2:30)
- **Visual:** Large data chart or table showing the Impact Metrics.
- **Narrative:** Present your metrics table. "By aligning with UN SDG Target [X], our app projects an offset of..." Explain how the tech facilitates this scale.

### Slide 5: The Team & Vision (2:30 - 3:00)
- **Visual:** Brief photos or titles of the founders/builders.
- **Narrative:** Explain why this team was capable of building this in 24 hours, and what the next feature milestone would be (e.g., migrating from mockDb to PostgreSQL).
